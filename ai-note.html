AI disclosure

How this portfolio was made · Updated October 2026

The short version

This site was built with Claude, Anthropic’s AI. Claude drafted the design brief from my existing portfolio, and I reviewed and edited it until it was mine. I wrote the case studies, with Claude as editor. Claude Design and Claude Code turned brief and copy into pages, and I reviewed and revised each one.

What’s mine

The words on the pages are mine, mostly. I wrote the near-final drafts of the case studies, weighed Claude’s suggested edits, and made the final edits myself. The decisions about what to show and what to leave out are mine too, including which images run and which rejected alternatives appear in the diagrams.

What Claude did

Claude drafted the brief from my existing site, its palette, type, and the way the case studies were told, then produced the HTML and CSS from it, drew the decision diagrams from my descriptions, checked color contrast, and flagged where the brief was ambiguous or where the output had deviated. When the output missed, I said so, and several of those corrections became rules in the brief for the rest of the site. The diagrams’ branching rule is one example: a rejected alternative branches from the decision it replaced, not from the start of the flow, so the reader sees what the choice was actually between.

The apps

How AI is used inside Lemon Pig and Wut Mutt, and what it isn’t used for, is covered in each case study and in the disclosure inside each app.
